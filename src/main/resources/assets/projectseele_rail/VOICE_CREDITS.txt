Project SEELE R65 railway announcements

Original Japanese announcements synthesized offline with Open JTalk and HTS Voice "Mei".
HTS Voice "Mei" was released by the MMDAgent Project Team, copyright 2009–2013
Nagoya Institute of Technology, Department of Computer Science, under CC BY 3.0.
https://www.mmdagent.jp/
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

The station, terminus, interchange and through-service recordings use the normal Mei voice at 1.03 speed,
normalized and encoded as mono Ogg Vorbis. The voice model is distributed separately.
